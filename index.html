About Us

A Stone Supplier Rooted in G-13, Islamabad

Hassan Marble and Tiles is a marble and granite supplier based on Street 589, G-13/2, Islamabad. We supply marble, granite and chips flooring, and take on custom inlay work — from simple borders to detailed floral designs.

We serve homes, mosques, offices and commercial spaces across Islamabad and Rawalpindi. Visit our yard to see the stone in person, or send us a message on WhatsApp — we are happy to help you choose.
